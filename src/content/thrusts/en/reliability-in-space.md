Perovskite devices in orbit face proton irradiation, thermal cycling and vacuum at the same time, and the usual way to find out how long one lasts is to irradiate it and wait. I build simulation-to-prediction chains that connect radiation transport physics to measurable device lifetime, so stability can be estimated before a device is ever flown.
